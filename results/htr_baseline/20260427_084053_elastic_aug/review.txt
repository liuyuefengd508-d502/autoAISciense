The main intervention is an online augmentation pipeline combining mild elastic distortion with a small affine transform. The authors compare three configurations: an 8-epoch no-augmentation baseline, a 16-epoch no-augmentation control, and a 16-epoch augmented run, with the goal of separating the effect of augmentation from the effect of longer training. The augmented run improves test CER and test loss relative to both no-augmentation runs, while WER remains very high and does not improve.",
